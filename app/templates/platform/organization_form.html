Provider model

Platform-managed Twilio by default

  • `AC...` and the platform auth token stay on the server for master-account access.
  • Each organization gets its own subaccount and messaging service.
  • Per-org Twilio secrets are stored encrypted in the database, not in `.env`.
  • BYO-Twilio is intentionally out of the default onboarding path.
